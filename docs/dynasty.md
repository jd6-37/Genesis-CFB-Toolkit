# Dynasty

The Dynasty section (`/dynasty`) keeps a multi-season record of the 138-team 2026 Season Edition or the 107-team 1994 Season Edition (see "The 1994 Season Edition" below): the app owns the players, the game plays the games, and the battery save carries each season's results back to the app.

Code: `src/core/dynasty/` (no DOM) and `src/tools/dynasty/` (the pages). Tests: `npm run test:dynasty -- ELEVATED_2026_138_DYNASTY SAVE.srm` (v2.0.1-test9 or later; the test marks the save for its dynasty) and `npm run test:dynasty94 -- ELEVATED_1994 SAVE.srm` (the 1994 Season Edition, v2.0.1-test29 or later).

## The 1994 Season Edition

User, 2026-11-15: "Make it compatible with our latest 1994 rom (Bill Walsh College Football 95 (USA) [Elevated 1994 Season v2.0.1-test29])". The 1994 edition is on the 2026 edition's season engine and dynasty support, so most of this page holds for both; `edition.ts` keeps what differs and `Dynasty.edition` says which a dynasty is (absent = 2026, the dynasties made before).

| | 2026 Season Edition | 1994 Season Edition |
|---|---|---|
| Teams | 138 | 107 (the 36 original teams, then CF97's order, Pacific last) |
| Save signature ($200001) | 0x6F | 0x94 |
| Season block | per-team tables at +$50 (power), +$164 (last week's ranks), +$1EE (user teams) | the same offsets, 107 entries used (checked in the emulator) |
| Dynasty header +0x10 | 0x0001008A | 0x0001006B (the sets 107 teams long; same addresses) |
| Regular season | 12 weeks | 11 weeks; 107 teams, so one team a week has a bye (53 games a week) |
| Postseason | title games, then the CFP (or BCS 2, none) | the 19 bowls of 1994 in one week (Bowls or None); no playoff picture |
| Champion | the last game's winner | the No. 1 team in the final poll once the season is over (bowls or not) |
| First season's rosters | the built-in CFB 27 file (`rosters.ts`) | the ROM's own (`create1994.ts`) |
| Transfer portal | yes | none (user, H-011: "transfer portal disabled for 94 rom"; `Edition.transfers`, `hasTransfers`) |

**Creating a 1994 dynasty** (`create1994.ts`, `createDynasty1994`):
- **The 32 per team** are the ROM's: the roster records' steps, the fine-ratings table at 0x2E0000 (offsets; the 1994 ROM has them for all 107 teams since test23) and the names table at 0x2C0000 (`readNamesTable`: class and name per slot). The app's numbers equal what Team Rosters shows.
- **Names:** a player the ROM has no name for keeps none (user: "keep names blank but do assign a class"; the Rosters lane finishes the names for the release). The pages call him by position and number (`playerName`: "HB #23"), and the season ROM's names set gives him his class and an empty name, as the ROM itself does. New players signed in the dynasty get names as in 2026, from the named players.
- **Classes:** the ROM's, unless it has none (class 5 or no record), or the slot is one of the 36 original teams' unnamed players, whose JR is a placeholder. A named original player whose JR the 1994 lists contradict takes the lists' class. The rest are given so each team's 32 move toward 2 FR, 6 SO, 12 JR and 12 SR (the ROM's named players' mix, `CLASS_MIX_1994`; user: "assign randomly to achieve a good balance of each class on each team"), the class each team is shortest of first.
- **Backups** (user: "We actually have full IRL rosters for most of the 94 teams. Let's use that for creating the backups"): for each slot, a real player of the school from the built-in 1994 rosters who isn't one of the ROM's named 32, of the slot's position (or a close one: HB/FB, DE/DL/OLB, CB/DB/S …), upperclassmen first. He brings his name, number (when his position may wear it and no teammate on his side has it; else the nearest allowed one), class, height, weight and hometown; his ratings are generated like the random bench's (`bench.ts`, stars by prestige, developed for his years). Where a school's list runs out (the lists of a few schools have 22-27 players), a generated player. Real backups are marked `srcId` 200000 + their row in the file; the ROM's 32 have 100000 + 32 × team + slot (both count as starting players for names, towns and letters).
- **Heights and hometowns:** for the ROM's named players, from the lists where the name is found; otherwise a height from the lists' players of the position, and a hometown from the school's own players in the lists, or mostly (60%) from towns in the school's state.
- **The related ratings** a lineup move needs (a lineman's Quickness for LB, a linebacker's Power for DE) aren't in the ROM: near his Speed or his Tackling, a little lower (Claude's pick).
- **Prestige:** the 1994 preseason AP poll (Top 25 and the others receiving votes) in its order, then the teams' OVRs (`startingPrestige1994`); CFB 27's prestige is 2026's.

**The built-in 1994 rosters** (`public/data/rosters-1994.csv`, `rosters1994.ts`): made by `npx tsx cli/rosters1994data.ts DIR` from the Rosters lane's lists (the owner's Mac folder `rosters-1994/rost94/*_rosters.csv`: Athlon, Lindy's and team guides (R*), StatMuse (T*, U1, U2)). 9,850 rows, 8,634 players after merging the same name at the same school (number, class, height and weight StatMuse first, position and hometown magazine first), all 107 schools; hometowns cleaned to the town and a two-letter state ("Non-US" abroad).

**Schedules:** 11 weeks (`DSchedule.weeks`; `weeksOf`), the ROM's conference weeks, and the 1994 protected rivalries (`PROTECTED_1994`, Claude's pick: the finales of that era in week 11, such as Nebraska-Oklahoma, Kansas-Missouri, North Carolina-Duke, Tennessee-Kentucky, BYU-Utah, and the other annual games of 1994). The schedule editor offers an opponent in a team's bye week (the bye then goes to whoever is left over) and doesn't move a game into a bye week.

**School data:** the tables keyed by school name (states, colors, academics, rivalries) also answer to the 1994 ROM's names (`SCHOOL_ALIASES`: California, Miami (Ohio), N. Illinois, NE Louisiana, SW Louisiana) and have Pacific (CA; orange and black; academics B, Claude's pick).

**Bowls in the app** (`derive.ts`): the bowl week (the season's last week, `bowlWeek`) is "Bowl Games"; from 1994 test31 the week before it is the SEC Championship Game ("SEC Championship"; its winner gets a conference title story; like the game, the app doesn't count it as a conference game). The first season can also be played with Game Setup's Real 1994 schedule (test30 on: Weeks 0-13, then the SEC game and the bowls); from the second season the game plays the dynasty's 11-week schedule. its games are the bowls in the ROM's order (`BOWLS_1994`, the order of its Week Schedule rows: Rose, Orange, Sugar, Cotton, Fiesta, Florida Citrus, Holiday, Gator, Peach, Sun, Hall of Fame, Freedom, Alamo, Copper, Aloha, Carquest, Liberty, Independence, Las Vegas). The front page shows them in place of the playoff picture; the stories have the bowl lineup, each bowl's result and the national champion of the final poll.

**Checked in the emulator** (Genesis Plus GX through a small libretro host, 2026-11-15, on test29 and again on test31): a 1994 dynasty's first-season ROM played to the end (11 weeks, on test31 the SEC Championship Game, the 19 bowls), the save imported (champion, bowls and stories as above), the offseason simulated and the 1995 ROM's New Season: it plays the dynasty's 11-week schedule (the import has no schedule warning) and the save carries the season-2 stamp; on test31 the whole 1995 season played (the SEC Championship Game and the bowls follow the dynasty's 11 weeks) and imported. A season with the Real 1994 schedule imports too (`test:dynasty94` on such a save). **Found:** the 1994 ROM sets the real 1994 preseason AP poll at every New Season, so a dynasty's 1995 season starts with 1994's poll; a ROM change (handoff H-010 in the claude.ai project).


## The battery save

RetroArch's Genesis Plus GX core writes a 65,456-byte `.srm`: cartridge save RAM from $200000, one data byte per word on the odd addresses, so file offset = address − $200000. A file of the data bytes alone (half the size) is read too.

- $200001: signature 0x6F. $200003: 0 once a season exists.
- Season block at $200E01, 0x1330 data bytes (the game copies it with the routines at 0x05B452 and 0x05B48C):

| Offset | Size | What |
|---|---|---|
| +0 | 1 | 0x5A |
| +1 | 1 | index of the last regular-season week |
| +2 | 1 | number of weeks (regular season and postseason) |
| +3 | 1 | flags: bit 0 postseason, bit 1 Real 2026 schedule, bit 2 CFP 24 |
| +6 | word | number of games |
| +8 | words | first game of each week, and one past the last |
| +$34 | 24 | playoff seeds (team numbers, seed 1 first) |
| +$50 | 138 words | power ratings; the Top 25 is the teams in power order, ties by team number |
| +$164 | 138 | each team's rank at the end of last week |
| +$1EE | 18 | the user's teams, one bit per team |
| +$200 | 4 per game | visitor score, visitor, home score, home. A score of FF = not played, FE = HOLD (a user's game); a team of FF = to be decided |

- Season stats at $203951: 138 records of 0xB8 data bytes, one per team, kept per roster slot (season totals, not per game). Labels from the game's Season Stats screens and its category table at 0x0DFB08:

| Category | Offset | Slots | Fields (w = word, sw = signed word, b = byte) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passing | 0 | 0 | att w, comp w, yds sw, int b, td b |
| Rushing | 8 | 0-3, 8 bytes each | att w, yds sw, long, short, fum, td |
| Receiving | 40 | 1-3, 9-14 | rec w, yds sw, long, drops, fum, td |
| Kicking | 112 | 15 | FGA, FGM, long, blocked, XPA, XPM |
| Punting | 118 | 31 | punts w, yds sw, long, inside 10 |
| Defense | 124 | 16-30, 4 bytes each | tackles w, sacks, int |

**Save layout 2** (H-033; 2026 test63 / 1994 test64 on; the claude.ai project's `bw95-manifest-spec.md`, section 2). `openSave` tells the layouts apart by the signature (`layoutOfSig`): 2026 0xE6, 1994 0xE4 (layout 1: 0x6F / 0x94). The season block is 0x1370 bytes: the dynasty stamp at +0x1330 as before, then the build stamp **ELVB** at +0x1340 (`readElvb` in `src/core/manifest.ts`: start build serial and number at New Season, last build serial and number on every season save, generation, save layout, edition, variant) and, at +0x1360, the **last-played ID** (H-037): the dynasty ID block of the ROM that saved the season last. Stats records are 169 bytes (stride 0x152 in the window): bytes 0-123 as above, then the 15 defenders in 3 bytes each: tackles bits 0-7; (tackles bits 8-9 << 6) | sacks; interceptions. A ROM with save layout 2 resets an older save once (no season), so a season can't be carried across the change.

**The ROM manifest** (`src/core/manifest.ts`, the same spec, section 1): `readManifest` reads "ELV1" at 0x2DFF00 (build name, serial, generation, mid-season-from, variant, the 16-byte entries). What the app does with it:
- `newerRomMessage`: a ROM with a required entry (flags bit 0) whose version, or a generation, this app doesn't know is refused at creation and when a season ROM is written ("this ROM is newer than this version of the Dynasty app").
- `DSeason.romBuild`: the base ROM's build, recorded at creation and at every season ROM download; `SaveImport.elvb`: the save's ELVB at import.
- The mid-season check at import (`midSeasonProblem`): the save's last build differs from its start build and the start is older than the last build's mid-season-from → a warning. Mid-season-from comes from the season's `romBuild` when it is that build, else from `KNOWN_BUILDS` (add a line for each mainline).
- H-037: the roster version warning uses the last-played ID when the save has one, so a season started on an earlier download and played on the newest gives no warning; one last played on an older download names its version.
- `hasFineRatings` answers from the FINE entry when there is a manifest.
- `npm run test:manifest -- ROM [SAVE …]` checks a mainline's manifest against the app (every required entry known, the build in `KNOWN_BUILDS`, the addresses the app uses) and reads saves made on it (layout 2, ELVB, packed defenders). The ROM lane can run it on each new mainline.

A record still all FF has never been cleared and reads as zeros. Passing and receiving add up across the league but not always per team (the game sometimes credits a catch without a completion).

Week names start at Week 0, always (user, 2026-11-26: "Let's use week 0 everywhere"; the game does the same from the ROM lane's H-061 build); after the regular season come the conference championships and then the playoff rounds (1, 2, 4 or 5 rounds for 2, 4, 12 or 24 teams).

## The dynasty

**Rosters: 64 per team** (user, 2026-11-12): the Active Roster (the 32 in the game) and a backup for each slot (`rosters.ts`). The 32 are the Rosters tool's CFB 27 conversion of the built-in ratings (what the ROM holds); the backups are the same conversion run again over the players left over, so each slot's backup plays its position, rated on the 32's scale (`rateAgainst` in `rosterCfb27.ts`: for each rating, the step of the converted player of the same role whose CFB 27 score is closest below his). The dynasty keeps only these players (about 8,500 at the start; K/P backups can be shared). This first season is the only use of the CFB 27 data (user, 2026-11-12): from then on the players' own Bill Walsh '95 ratings are the truth ("Ratings" below). In the first season the user can replace every team's backups with generated players (`bench.ts`, Manage page). Pages show all 64, clearly split into "Active Roster" and "Backups" (user, 2026-11-12); a backup's page shows his ratings if he played, ranked against the Active Roster players.

`Dynasty` (`model.ts`) holds the teams (school, nickname, city and uniform colors from the ROM), every player (name, position, class FR/SO/JR/SR/SR+, jersey, height, weight, hometown, OVR, his Bill Walsh '95 ratings, a skin tone 0-3 for the app's portraits, status) and the seasons. A season has its year, the conferences and their names from the ROM, the 32 roster slots of each team (which dynasty player the ROM has in each slot, so slot stats belong to players), each slot's Bill Walsh '95 ratings (the six 0-15 steps the game uses; at creation they are the Rosters tool's CFB 27 conversion, which for v2.0.0 is exactly what the ROM holds in all 4,416 slots) and the imported saves. Player pages show only these game ratings; a player who isn't in one of his team's 32 slots has none.

- Created from the ROM and the built-in CFB 27 ratings, with the Rosters tool's own CFB 27 conversion choosing each team's 32. A redshirted player starts one class up.
- The dynasty file is version 2 (Bill Walsh '95 ratings); version 1 dynasties (CFB 27 ratings, earlier test versions) can't be opened.

### Ratings

`ratings.ts` (user, 2026-11-12: "Why are you still using the cfb27 data?! That was only used to convert the first roster"). Every player keeps a number (0-100, one decimal) for Speed, Quickness, Power, Awareness, Hands, Range, Accuracy, Blocking and Tackling, and the game's Weight in pounds (the OL tweak's pounds are added on screen as before). Players also keep the related rating group's numbers for position changes: defensive linemen a Quickness, linebackers a Power (from the same conversion, rated as the other role).

- **Fine ratings** (`fine.ts`; Elevated 2026 v2.0.1-test13 on; user, 2026-11-12: "We've completed the fine-ratings mod … do whatever you need to do in the app to show those ratings, adjust progression, recruits, etc."; "I'm not worried about making old saves compatible"):
  - The game plays with a fine value per rating, 16 × step + offset. The step (0-15) is in the roster record; the offset (−8..+7) is in a table. Team Rosters shows ⌊fine × 100 / 240⌋, at most 99.
  - The app's number × 2.4 is the fine value (`fineOf`, 100 = 240). The record gets the nearest step and the table the rest (`splitFine`; before the mod a number counted as the step it had reached). Weight: pounds − 140 is the fine value.
  - The app shows the number the game shows (`shownOf`, `shownFine`): 0-99.
  - **First season:** the Active Roster's offsets are read from the ROM's 2026 table at 0x2E0000 when the record has the conversion's steps (all 4,416 slots on test14), so the app shows what Team Rosters shows. Backups aren't in the ROM: theirs are drawn evenly (a step's average stays the step). The app's numbers stop at 100 (the game shows 99 either way), but since H-031 (2026-11-18) the first season's slots keep the ROM's offsets above step 15 (fine up to 247, as the game plays them), so the impact pick and the season ROM's offsets table match the game; a player's own number is still at most 100, and later seasons' slots come from his number. Dynasties created before that dropped those offsets (112 of 4,416 slots in 2026). An offset below step 0 counts as 0, as in the game. On a ROM without the mod, all offsets are drawn. (`hasFineRatings` finds the mod by the snap's jump at 0x06FAF4; since v2.0.1-test20 that jump goes to the impact players' hook first, which goes on into the fine-ratings code. Until 2026-11-15 the check missed that, so dynasties made on test20 and later drew all their first-season offsets. Since 2026 test53 / 1994 test54 the impact players' zone odds (`impzone.py`, 0x2D7700) sit in front of the impact hook; the check follows up to four hops through these known hooks.)
  - **Later seasons:** every slot's step and offset come from the player's number (`bwSlotOf`); `BwSlot.offs` holds the offsets. The season ROM gets them as a set in the dynasty area (see "The season ROM"). The game doesn't read that set yet (up to test14 the snap and Team Rosters use no offsets for a dynasty roster, so dynasty seasons play and show whole steps); handoff `claude/bw95-dynasty-fine-handoff.md`.
  - 30 CPU-only seasons with the fine model: the league stays at the 2026 level (median team best-22 OVR 79.4-80.8 every year, starters 76-78).
- **v8 dynasties** (H-039; `Dynasty.ratingsV: 8`, made on a ROM with the v8 tables: 2026 test60 / 1994 test61 on): stored numbers are still fine ÷ 2.4, but every page shows the v8 number (`shownAt`, `shownInSlot`: the same tables as the game's Team Rosters, so a CFB 27 Speed 91 shows 91 at every position), OVRs use `OVR_FORMULAS_V8`, the edit form takes the shown number (`valueOfShown`, through the curve), and development and recruits move in shown points (`toScale` / `fromScale`, the curve reversed), so +1 is the same at every position. In the first season every Active Roster slot takes the ROM's steps and offsets (the ROM's built-in ratings are the same conversion); backups keep the conversion's. Dynasties made before keep the old numbers (user, 2026-11-18: "Start new dynasties"). The app has one dynasty open, so the scale is set when one opens or is made (`setRatingScale`).
- **OVR** (`ovrOf`, `ovrAt`, `playerOvr`): the OVR formulas below on the shown numbers (from the fine values), at his position's role, or a slot's role for the depth chart.
- **Progress:** development raises the numbers and every point plays. The player page shows each rating's number, a 15-part meter filled to the fine value, and his rank among the league's players at the position.
- **Depth charts** (`depth.ts`): each slot goes to the player of its position with the best OVR at the slot's role, the Active Roster first, then the backups; then close positions (HB: FB, WR; DE: the other DE, DT; CB: FS, SS …), then anyone. A kicker can also punt when a team has no punter.
- **New players** (`makeRecruit`, `recruitTemplates`): modeled on a league player of the position at the star rating's place (5★ top 3%, 4★ next 17%, 3★ 35-80%, 2★ 5-35%, 1★ bottom 5%; walk-ons bottom 8%), younger by 7-13 OVR points (spread over the ratings his OVR counts, Speed at 40%), every rating ±3, to one decimal (not whole numbers or steps). Names and hometowns from the starting players. In a six-season run (2026-2032, random classes) the starters' average OVR stays at 75-78.

### Headshots

`portrait.ts` draws, `headshot.ts` makes a player's look, `coachLook.ts` a coach's; `Headshot` and `CoachHeadshot` in the UI put them in a canvas scaled up with crisp pixels. The picture isn't stored: the look is made from the player on every page view (so snapshots show it too). **Appearance editing** (user, 2026-11-12): `DPlayer.look` keeps only the parts the user changed (`LookEdit`; `editLook` in edit.ts, also the skin tone); `lookOf` applies them over the generated look. Allowed any time, any player; "Back to his original look" clears it.

**The 16-bit style** (user, 2026-11-18: "These look like simple 8-bit graphics but we should be shooting for 16-bit era graphics"; reviewed on the "Dynasty headshots: 16-bit direction" page over several rounds; approved "bingo"): a 48 × 48 painterly pixel portrait, the face filling the frame, flat planes of color with slightly irregular edges and no dithering, hue-shifted ramps (shadows toward red, lights toward yellow; light skin's shadows less saturated; dark hair with cool blue-gray highlights), a darker edge on the shadow side only, light from the left, eyes looking at the camera, a mouth with lips and a shadow under the lower lip, the jersey in the team's main color with a crew collar in its second color (gray and white without a team), and the flat #555555 of real player photos behind (user: "this color gray seems to work well IRL"). Athletes: traps and a strong neck.
- **From the player:** skin tone (four), build by position (wide for OL and DL; medium for HB, FB, TE and LB; slim for the rest).
- **From his ID, the earlier draws** (so existing players keep their hair): hair style from `GENERATED_STYLES` (never grow or reorder it), hair color, facial hair (freshmen and sophomores stubble at most), expression, and eye black, now about 1 in 20 (user, 2026-11-18: "eye black on 30% is way too much. Make it like 5%").
- **From a second draw of its own:** jaw (narrow, medium, wide) and neck (thin, medium, thick) by position (`jawNeckFor`; user: "wide jaw + thick neck more common with lineman. Narrow jaw + thin neck never on lineman ... wide jaw + thin neck should probably never be used. Narrow jaw + thick neck looks great though"): linemen thick necks (85%, else medium) with wide 55 / medium 30 / narrow 15 jaws; HB, FB, TE and LB necks thick 40 / medium 50 / thin 10, jaws medium 50 / wide 25 / narrow 25; the rest necks thin 40 / medium 50 / thick 10, jaws narrow 40 / medium 50 / wide 10; a wide jaw on a thin neck becomes medium. Eye color by skin tone (blue, green, gray and hazel mostly on the lighter tones, brown on all), earrings (15%), a chain (8%).
- **New players** (`DPlayer.lookV` 2: recruits, walk-ons, added players and a new dynasty's players, from 2026-11-18): the hair style from the full list (short, buzz cut, fade, side part, flat-top, high-top, curls, twists, afro, locs long and short, cornrows, braids, flow, mullet, man bun, mohawk, bald), weighted by skin tone with mullet and man bun rare; a few red-haired light-skinned players; a few mustaches.
- **Age** (players young unless edited): middle-aged faces get forehead and smile lines and graying temples; older ones deeper lines, bags under the eyes, hair 60% gray (players) and a receding hairline (unless the coach's hair is set otherwise).
- **Editor:** skin tone, hair, hair color (adds red and gray), facial hair (adds mustache), expression, eye color, age, jaw, neck, eye black, earrings, chain.
- **Left out on purpose** (user, 2026-11-18): chinstrap beard, durag, nose strip, headband, a coach's headset.

### Editing

`edit.ts`, `EditPlayer.tsx` (user, 2026-11-12: "full capabilities like we have in the mod toolkit"; every team). In the offseason and before a season's first results are imported (`editLock`):
- **Player page:** Edit player: name, position, class, number, height, weight, hometown, potential, team (moves him), and his ratings (0-100; the form shows the number the game shows; Game weight in pounds). Release him (he leaves the dynasty).
- **Team page:** Add a player at a position (a generated walk-on, then edit him).
- **Manage, Players (CSV):** download every player (ID, Team, names, Position, Class, Jersey, Height, Weight, Hometown, Home State, OVR, Potential, Game Weight and the nine ratings); import an edited file: rows with an ID change that player (only what differs), rows without one add a player (Team and Position needed), a cleared Team releases him. The changes and any problems are listed first; nothing changes until Apply.
- In the preseason the season follows at once: teams whose players came or went get a new depth chart, the others keep their lineups with the new ratings; team OVRs move by the starters' change; the ROM must be downloaded again (roster version + 1). In the offseason the depth charts come at its end.
- Each import keeps the save's data bytes (base64). One import per number of completed weeks, the newest kept. An import with fewer results than the last is refused unless allowed; one whose games differ from the last import's is refused as another season. Teams the save marks as the user's are added.
- Everything shown (records, standings, ranks, leaders, records, champions, stories) is derived from the imports (`derive.ts`, `news.ts`), so the same saves always give the same pages.

### Team and player OVRs

- Team OFF and DEF are the ROM's own (what Game Setup shows), read when the dynasty is created.
- A player's OVR comes from his six Bill Walsh '95 ratings: OVR = base + scale × the weighted average of the ratings as the game shows them (0-100), by roster role, capped at 0-99 (`src/core/dynasty/ovr.ts`). Only ratings the game uses at that position count: Weight is mass, not quality; kickers' and punters' Range isn't used for kicks (their Quick is kick distance). Linemen's Block counts: it keeps them on their feet after hard hits. QB Speed is set to 15% (the QB runs in the game; CFB 27's Overall ignores it). The shares, base and scale were fitted to the CFB 27 Overall of the 4,416 starters of v2.0.0:

### Team colors

The pages use each school's official primary and secondary colors (`src/core/dynasty/schoolColors.ts`, all 138 schools by the ROM's school name); a school missing there falls back to the ROM's team color (the jersey when that is near black or white). The ROM's colors are tuned for the Genesis palette and the uniforms, so they often read wrong on a page (LSU too blue, Iowa blue, Georgia too dark). Marks, meters and charts on white use the primary, or the secondary when the primary is too light (Iowa's gold → black). A game's split picture uses the second team's secondary when both primaries are close (Louisiana and USC reds).

### Storage and the dynasty file

IndexedDB database `genesis-cfb-dynasty`: store `dynasties` (the dynasty and when it changed) and store `art` (each team's logo, helmet and wordmark drawn from the user's ROM). The dynasty file is the dynasty as JSON with `app: "genesis-cfb-toolkit"`, `tool: "dynasty"`, `version: 1`; it never carries the art. A file from a newer version is refused.

### Lineups

`src/core/dynasty/lineup.ts` (user, 2026-11-12). A team has 64 spots: the 32 slots (the Active Roster) and each slot's backup, named QB2, HB3 and HB4 (behind HB1 and HB2), FB2, SE2, FL2, WR5, WR6, TE3, TE4, LT2 … RT2, LDE2, DT3, DT4, RDE2, LOLB2, MLB3, MLB4, ROLB2, CB5 to CB8, SS2, FS3, FS4, K2, P2 (`backupLabel`: the family's size plus the slot's number). The labels are the game's from v2.0.1-test17 (WR3, WR4, CB1 to CB4; handoff H-005, 2026-11-13); before that the app showed WR1, WR2, LCB1, RCB1, LCB2, RCB2, with backups WR3, WR4, LCB3, LCB4, RCB3, RCB4. Dynasty files store slot numbers, not labels, so older files open and show the new labels. On a user team's page the Pos column is a menu, grouped by position with ★ on the Active Roster spots; choosing a spot swaps the player with the one there (who must be able to play the spot left; an Active Roster spot is never left empty).

- **Which spots:** any spot of his position (by `DPlayer.pos`, plus the kind of spot he already holds), and one related position: HB↔FB, FB↔TE, DE↔OLB, OLB↔MLB, K↔P. No CB/S or DE/DT (user: so nobody signs corners to play safety). A change to another position sets his `pos` (LE/RE, LOLB/ROLB by the spot) and his CFB 27-style OVR, and is recorded in `posChange` with the season: once a season, so a TE needs a season at FB before HB. In that season he can still move between the spots of both positions (back to his old one undoes the change).
- **Ratings:** he plays with his own numbers (ratings.ts), as the new slot's rating group uses them.
- **Team OVRs** move by the change in `starterOvr` of the team's starters (season one's are the ROM's own).
- **When:** user teams only, outside the offseason, before any imported save has a result (stats belong to slots). `DSeason.lineupEdited` shows a reminder to download the season's ROM again (cleared by the download). The next offseason's `depthChart` sets every lineup again from the players' positions.

## The season ROM

`seasonRom` (`offseason.ts`) writes a season's 32 per team (jersey numbers and game ratings, on the 2026 record of each slot) and the team OVRs into a copy of the user's ROM, checksums fixed.

- **Only ROMs with the dynasty support and Team Rosters' names** (Elevated 2026 Season Edition v2.0.1-test9 on, user 2026-11-12: the mark `BW95DYN` + version at `0x2B0000`, the 2026 names table `NAM1` at `0x2C0000` and the names view's row hook at `0x06AFC0`; `hasDynastyHooks` in `dynastyArea.ts`); older ROMs and saves aren't supported before the first public release (user, 2026-11-12: "things are changing fast and big"). Everything goes into the dynasty area `0x3E0000`-`0x3FFFFF`, which Season mode reads and Exhibition never does. Header: the ID block (`DYN1`, dynasty ID = FNV-1a of the dynasty's app ID, season number = season index + 1, roster version, export time in Unix seconds), format word 1, team count 138, then the addresses of the roster set (`0x3E0100`, 138 × 32 game records), the OVR table (`0x3E4600`, `OVR1` + OFF/DEF bytes), the sim words (`0x3E4800`, `simFromOvr`) and (+0x20) the names set (`0x3E5000`, `NAM1`: team count, a long per team, then per slot class byte 0-4, first name, NUL, last name, NUL; Team Rosters' names view in v2.0.1-test9 on; season one's set equals the ROM's own 2026 names table at `0x2C0000`). The ID block is written last. The area must be all `0xFF` or hold a valid header (`DYN1`, `0x0001008A` at +0x10); the 2026 data stays as it is. Season one's sets equal the ROM's own rosters and OVRs.
- **Roster version:** 1, then one more when the lineup or the schedule changes after the last download (`DSeason.rosterVersion`, lineup and schedule editors).
- **Fine-ratings offsets:** header +0x24 → `FRC1` + 138 × 32 × 3 bytes at 0x3FB000, six nibbles a slot in the roster record's order (−8..+7), the format of the ROM's 2026 table at 0x2E0000 (`putFineOffsets`, `fineAddress`). The names set may now run to 0x3FB000.
- **Schedule** (from the second season): header +0x28 → `SCH1` set at 0x3FF000 (see "Schedules"); 0xFFFFFFFF in the first season.
- **The save stamp:** New Season copies the ID block into the season block's +0x1330. `importSave` only takes stamped saves: another dynasty's or season's are refused (this also refuses last season's save in a new season), and an older roster version than the season's gives a warning (the season was started before the last lineup change). Exception: the first season's save without a stamp (started with the plain 2026 ROM, which has the same rosters as the app's first-season ROM) is taken with a warning (user's save, 2026-11-12); later seasons need the stamp.
- The Manage page has a Download button for the current season's ROM (the first season's too, so its save gets the stamp); the Offseason page has one for the next season; the lineup editor reminds the user to download again.

## Schedules

From the second season the app makes each season's schedule (`schedule.ts`; user, 2026-11-12: "Generate each year, and then the user can edit … Basically use same rules"). The first season plays the one chosen in the game (Real 2026 or Random).

- **Rules** (the season engine's Random schedule, `GEN`):
  - 12 weeks, and every team plays every week.
  - Each conference plays its conference games as circle-method rounds of a shuffled order. The number of games and the first week come from the ROM's CWN/CWS tables, read when the dynasty is created and kept in `DSeason.confWeeks`. Dynasties made before that use the released values.
  - Everyone else is paired at random: another conference first, then no repeats.
  - Home and away: rivalry games switch sites from last season. Otherwise the team with fewer home games hosts, evened out so every team has 5-7.
- **Protected rivalries** (`PROTECTED` in `rivalries.ts`, 45 pairs; user: "Alabama should always play Auburn (ideally, last week of season). Michigan should always play Ohio State, even though there are 18 teams in the Big Ten").
  - Inside a conference, the rounds are picked so they hold the conference's protected games.
  - 20 season finales go in week 12 (Iron Bowl, The Game, Egg Bowl, Clean, Old-Fashioned Hate, Army-Navy …). When a finale isn't there, its two teams leave their week-12 games, and those games move to a week where both teams are free.
  - Protected games across conferences go in a week where both teams are free.
  - Which pairs are protected, and which are finales, was Claude's pick.
  - Checked: every protected game on the schedule and every finale in week 12, over 30 schedules.
- **Editing** (`SchedulePage.tsx`, `/dynasty#schedule/<team>`, linked from a user team's page and the new-season ROM window; user: "We don't need to edit cpu teams, only the dynasty team(s)"; "I would like to be able to edit conference games too"):
  - Per week, the user can change his team's opponent, conference teams included (`setOpponent`). His old opponent then plays the new opponent's old opponent, so everyone still plays once a week.
  - He can move the game to another week (`moveGame`, two such swaps; choosing an opponent he already plays moves that game), or switch home and away (`flipHome`).
  - The page shows conference games, home games, rivalries, games played twice and protected rivalries no longer on the schedule.
  - Changes are saved as they are made. Under the list (user, 2026-11-13: "This is very confusing as to what i should be doing … I wasnt sure what button to click. and when i did, i didn't know what to do next"): "When you're done" says so and gives the next step (Download the ROM, Depth chart); "Start over" has "Throw this schedule away…", which asks in a window before it makes another schedule for everyone. Before, the only button under the list was "Make a new schedule…", which read like the way to finish.
  - Locked once the season has an import. A change after the ROM download raises the roster version and asks for a new download (like a lineup change).
- **In the ROM:** header +0x28 points to the schedule set at 0x3FF000. The set is `SCH1`, weeks, then per week a count and visitor/home bytes, the format of the ROM's real 2026 table (see "The season ROM"). The game needs a change to play it (New Season reads it instead of making one); the handoff is `claude/bw95-dynasty-schedule-handoff.md` in the claude.ai project. Until then New Season ignores it.
- **Import:** a save whose regular-season games aren't the dynasty's schedule gets a warning. The results count as played.
- Before the season's first import, the pages show the dynasty's schedule (unplayed games).

- **Preseason Heisman favorites** (`heismanPre.ts` `preseasonHeisman`; user, 2026-11-18: "Could we make a pre-season list of Heisman favorites? ## Season awards and the records book

**Season awards** (`awards.ts`, `AwardsPage.tsx`, `/dynasty#awards/<year>`; user, 2026-11-12: "Heisman, All-Conference and All-American teams, worked out from the stats"). Handed out once a season's last game is in. Every Active Roster player gets a score at his role: QB passing yards / 20 + TD × 4 − INT × 2 + rushing yards / 10 + rushing TD × 6; HB and FB rushing and receiving yards / 10 + TD × 6; WR and TE receiving yards / 10 + TD × 6 + catches / 4; offensive linemen (no stats) OVR + wins / 2; DE, DT and LB tackles + sacks × 3 + INT × 4; CB and S the same with INT × 5; K field goals × 3 − misses + extra points / 2; P yards a punt (10 punts or more). Stats and OVR together (user, 2026-11-12: "stats are very important in the decision-making, they aren't everything"; "they all should take overall into effect … all conference and all American teams … a little bit more stats based"): each player's stat score and OVR are ranked against everyone at his award spot (0 last to 1 first); only the leaders by stats are considered (top 10 for a position award, the top 4 per place, at least 6, for a team), and among them the best blend wins: 60% stats and 40% OVR for the Heisman and the position awards, 70/30 for the All-American and All-Conference teams (`AWARD_STATS_WEIGHT`, `TEAM_STATS_WEIGHT`). The stats part is three quarters the stat rank and one quarter how close to the spot's best (√ of score / best; 2026-11-27: a 1,608-yard, 84 OVR back led the Heisman race but missed the Doak Walker top 5 behind 89+ OVR backs with ~650 yards, because the leaders' ranks are all near 1; user: "I'd still make OVR matter … but someone with those stats deserves top 5". A back with 2.2× the next one's stats at 84 OVR now lands 3rd-4th, behind 90 OVR backs; `test:dynasty` checks it). The Heisman blends the top 15 of the Heisman watch (stats and team wins) with their OVR rank. Each line shows his OVR. Teams: QB 1, RB 2, WR 3, TE 1, OL 5, DL 4, LB 3, CB 2, S 2, K 1, P 1. All-American first and second team (nobody on both), each conference's first team (independents left out), the Heisman (the next four are finalists) and the best quarterback, running back, receiver, offensive lineman, defensive player and kicker. The player page lists his **Honors**; History links to the awards.

**Records book** (`records.ts`, `RecordsPage.tsx`, `/dynasty#records[/<team>]`, linked from History; the single-season records moved there from History). The whole league or one school (a school's records count only what was done there): single-season and career top 10 for the `RECORD_STATS` stats, and team records (top 5): most wins and points in a season, fewest points allowed in a season (finished seasons only), most points in a game and the biggest wins.

## Names and skin tones

`appearance.ts` (user, 2026-11-12). Players are White or Black in the app; that decides the skin tone and, for generated players, which names fit together. Nothing of it goes into the ROM (the game has one skin tone); the tones are for the app's portraits.

- **Share by position** (`BLACK_SHARE`, the user's numbers): QB 45%, HB 95.5, FB 47, WR 81.1, TE 35.4, LT 48.8, LG 49.5, C 34.3, RG 47.9, RT 48.7, LE 75.9, RE 70, DT 73.5, LOLB 75.1, MLB 69.6, ROLB 73.4, CB 98.2, FS 89.6, SS 89.2, K 3.8, P 4.8.
- **Skin tones** (`SKIN_TONES`, `DPlayer.skin` 0-3): Light and Tan for White players, Brown and Dark for Black players, half and half.
- **Name odds** (`nameOdds.json`, built by `cli/nameodds.ts`): for every first and last name in the CFB 27 file, how likely it's a Black player's: the US Census 2010 surname table (public domain) and Tzioumis (2018) first names (Harvard Dataverse, CC0), as copied in the `surgeo` package on PyPI. Names they don't have (many modern first names, hyphenated and rare surnames) are estimated from the other half of the names and the positions of the CFB 27 players who have them, pulled toward the average when few do.
- **Starting players** (`assignBlack`): per position, the share of the position's players, chosen by how their names point plus random noise (a logistic draw), so names lean the choice without fixing it.
- **Generated players** (`makeRecruit`): White or Black by the position's share, then a first and a last name weighted by how often they appear among CFB 27 players and how well they fit (count × odds, or × 1 − odds); never a real CFB 27 player's full name. Only names the game can show well: first names up to 10 characters, last names up to 12 (`MAX_FIRST`, `MAX_LAST`), in the game's font (A-Z a-z 0-9 ' - . space).
- **In the ROM** (the season ROM's names set): every name goes through `gameText` (accents dropped, other characters out, at most 23 characters, the room Team Rosters has for a last name).

## Prestige

`src/core/dynasty/prestige.ts`. Every school has 1-6 stars per season (`DSeason.prestige`). Scale (user, 2026-11-12): final poll top 10 = 6, top 20 = 5, top 35 = 4, top 70 = 3, top 100 = 2, else 1; when the season ends a school moves at most one star toward the tier it finished in. Start: College Football 27's prestige at launch (0-5 in half stars, `CFB27_PRESTIGE`); schools are ranked by it (ties by team OVR in the ROM) and given the tier of their place, so the first season has 10 six-star, 10 five-star, 15 four-star, 35 three-star, 30 two-star and 38 one-star programs. Recruiting and the portal read prestige as (stars − 1) / 5.

## The offseason

`src/core/dynasty/offseason.ts`, pure steps that each return a new dynasty; seeded, so the same choices give the same offseason. `Dynasty.offseason` holds the step, the seed, the events (for the news and the next season's `events`) and the players as they were at the start (Start the offseason over).

1. **startOffseason** (needs every game played; the app has no way to end a season early, user 2026-11-12): SR+ leave; seniors go pro (chance (OVR − 80) / 14, up to 85%), else come back as SR+ (30%) or graduate; juniors go pro (chance (OVR − 84) / 12, up to 70%). Everyone else moves up a class. Development waits for the end of the offseason (user, 2026-11-12: training progression "should happen after recruiting (to everyone except for HS recruits) … just prior to the final depth chart phase. It's basically the end result of the offseason training"): it runs with the cuts (step 4, `trainPlayers`), for every returning player still on a team (transfers at their new school, with its training focus); new signees and walk-ons don't develop that year. So the projected depth chart, transfer decisions, recruiting needs and cuts all see last season's ratings. Development: gain = (potential − OVR) × rate (FR 0.45, SO 0.40, JR 0.35, SR 0.30) × random 0.5-1.5, +1 for one of the team's 32, ± noise. The gain goes to the ratings his position's OVR counts, as numbers, shared out by each rating's pace (user, 2026-11-12: "SPD shouldn't be able to progress as much as AWR"): Awareness 1.4, Hands/Blocking/Tackling/Accuracy 1.2, Power 1.0, Range 0.8, Quickness 0.7, Speed 0.5 (Offseason rules, Development), scaled so the OVR still moves by the gain; a rating that reaches 100 or its yearly cap passes the rest to the others, and what none can take is lost (`develop`, `paceOf`, `capOf`). **Yearly caps** (user, 2026-11-12: "+30 AWR? Doesn't that seem like A LOT? +13 SPD?" … "yeah cap them"): at most Awareness +18, Power/Hands/Blocking/Tackling/Accuracy +14, Quickness/Range +10, Speed +6 rating points a year (rules; training focus moves them like the paces). Claude first proposed +10/+7/+5/+3, but in the 30-season run those made the league sink (median team best 22 from 80 to 67, since OVR moves only about half as fast as the ratings and recruits start 7-13 below); the caps above are about the old top-10% year, so they only trim the outliers (one offseason: Awareness up to +54, Quickness and Accuracy up to +60, Speed up to +27), and the league stays at the 2026 level (median about 80, starters about 77, from 2031 to 2056). Caps are in rating points, so they work the same with one-point ratings (the fine-ratings test ROM). Bigger players also put on 0-6 lb; see "Ratings". **Training focus** (`training.ts`; user, 2026-11-12): on the Offseason page, before the offseason, each of your teams splits 100 points over Strength program (Power, Range), Speed development (Speed, Quickness), Technique (Hands, Blocking, Tackling, Accuracy) and Game knowledge (Awareness), at most 40 in one area (user: "maybe we max at 40 at ±20%"). 25 points = the normal paces; 40 makes an area's ratings grow 20% faster, 0 makes them 20% slower (straight lines in between; `trainingFactor`). The year's OVR gain doesn't change, so the focus shapes the player, not how good he gets (an HB's +4 year with Speed development at 40: SPD +5.4 instead of +4.9, AWR +12.2 instead of +13.6). The page doesn't say which ratings an area affects (user: "to keep a little mystery"); the Offseason rules do (`trainingMax`, `trainingEffect`). Computer teams train evenly (user: "cpu stays at 25 each for now"). Kept per team in `Dynasty.training` until changed. In a 30-season CPU-only run team strength settles as before (median best 22 about 83.5 from 2030 on); about 3,300 ratings sit at 100 by then, mostly upperclassmen's Awareness and Speed (was about 1,600 with even paces). Potential is hidden: given at the first offseason (OVR + 6-18 for a FR … 0-3 for an SR+) or by stars when signed.
2. **runPortal**: nobody leaves too soon for playing time (user, 2026-11-12: the game can't give a backup a few snaps). Active Roster players never enter; backups only after two seasons as backups: 10%, +10% when the starter ahead of him stays, +10% for a senior (`transferChance`). So the first offseason has no transfers. Teams that need the position take them, best players first, stronger programs first in line for the good ones, at most four per team. Nobody takes him: he leaves the dynasty.
3. **runSigningDay**: each team signs 64 − roster (at most 25). Position: the biggest need against the targets, two per slot in the game (QB 2, HB 4, FB 2, WR 8, TE 4, each OL spot 2, LE 2, RE 2, DT 4, LOLB 2, MLB 4, ROLB 2, CB 8, FS 4, SS 2, K 2, P 2 = 64). Then walk-ons for any position with fewer players than slots (`fillMinimums`). Stars by prestige (last season's rank, record, title, roster strength). A recruit is made from the league's own players of the position ("New players" under "Ratings"), with a new name and a US hometown from the starting players. Class points: 5★ 100, 4★ 60, 3★ 30, 2★ 12, 1★ 4.
4. **applyCuts** ("Make the cuts and train"): down to 64 when a team has more (rare: walk-ons for position minimums), lowest value first (OVR + half the room to potential + years left), never below one per slot. User teams choose (the suggestion is checked). Then walk-ons up to 64, where the need is biggest. Then the offseason's training (`trainPlayers`, step 1); the next step's page shows each user team's most improved players. **Training results** (`TrainingPage.tsx`, `trainingResults` and `trainingYears` in career.ts; user, 2026-11-12: "a Training Results page, roster ordered by most improved"): `/dynasty#training/<team>-<year>`, linked from that step ("All training results") and the team page. Any team and any past offseason: every returning player (last season's year record against this season's, or his current ratings while the offseason is at its last step), biggest OVR gain first, with each rating's change ("AWR +30", weight in pounds) and the school a transfer came from; new signees and walk-ons aren't listed.
5. **finishOffseason**: jersey numbers (below), the Active Roster and the backups per team (`depth.ts`: the best OVR at each spot), their game ratings (the steps of their own numbers) and team OVRs (2026: from the starters' OVRs, `starterOvr` on the `v3` scale; 1994: `teamOvr94.ts`, the ROM lane's ovrfit94 method: each unit's mean z of its slots' five non-weight fine ratings against all 107 teams, scaled to last season's OVRs' mean and spread, 63-99, which gives 1994 test36's own OVRs back within 1 point), and the next season. Lineup changes and edits move a 1994 team's OVRs by the change that method sees (`shiftTeamOvrs1994`).

**Full recruiting** (`src/core/dynasty/recruiting.ts`; chosen on the Offseason page instead of random classes, each offseason):
- **Who enters the portal** (`portalEntries`): the same rule as the random version (`transferChance`: backups after two seasons). They become prospects; their team can't easily get them back (fit × 0.3).
- **The pool** (`startRecruiting`): about 1.4 prospects per open spot in the country (spots = 64 − roster, at most 25), positions in proportion to the country's needs, stars from a national spread, made like the random classes' recruits; each prospect wants playing time, prestige and being close to home in random proportions (portal players mostly playing time).
- **A week** (`runRecruitingWeek`): every team spends 1,000 points, at most 60 on one prospect, and makes offers. Interest gained = points × (0.5 + fit) × (1.2 with an offer, else 0.6) × reach × noise. Fit = his priorities · (playing time he'd get by depth at his position, the school's prestige, closeness: same state 1, same region 0.6, else 0.25). Reach = 1 − 0.2 × (his stars − (prestige stars − 1)), 0.3-1.15. He commits when the leading school (that offered and has a spot) reaches the threshold for his stars (1★ 60 … 5★ 320; portal players 70% of it) and leads the second by 25%, and not before week 2 (3★), 3 (4★) or 4 (5★). A committed 4-5★ prospect can flip to a school two stars more prestigious that outbids his school by 50%. CPU teams target their needs within their star range (up to 2 + prestige × 4 stars), points to the best targets (score squared).

**Model** (`coaches.ts`): `Dynasty.coaches` (index = id), and `DSeason.coach` per team (the head coach's id that season). A coach has `team` (head coach of; null in the pool), `status` (coach, pool, retired), `poolNote` ("OC at Alabama", "Not coaching"), five `ratings` 0-99, `prestige` 1-5, `irl` (his real seasons) and `manageXp` (next step). **Ratings** (Claude's method, marked on the pages as made by the app): a target OVR from his head coaching record at the edition's own schools (win share pulled toward .500 by 12 games: 60 + (pct − 0.5) × 70), a record below I-A / FBS counting for less (57 + (pct − 0.5) × 40, at most 74, weighted 0.4 per game), + 3.5 per national title, + conference titles (up to 5), + up to 4 for years; a coordinator with no head coaching record 55-68 by his years. OVR = (Passing + Running + Defense + 1.25 × Recruiting + 1.25 × Development) / 5.5. Prestige: 86+ 5 stars, 78+ 4, 70+ 3, 62+ 2, else 1; a national champion at least 4. Results: 2026 head coaches 52-95 (median 66; Kirby Smart 95), 1994 52-90 (median 64; Tom Osborne 90). A birth year the lists don't have, or that can't be right (younger than 22 at his first season), is estimated from his first season (marked "estimated").

**Tests:** `cli/coachchecks.ts`, run by `test:dynasty` and `test:dynasty94`: one head coach per team in every season, ratings and ages in range, a spread of OVRs, the pool's notes, no real season from the edition's year, the coaches don't change the season ROM, and the next season after an offseason has the head coaches.

### Part 2: goals, the hot seat, XP and the coaching carousel (`carousel.ts`, `CarouselStep.tsx`)

- **Goals** (`goalsFor`, `goalResults`): from the school's prestige that season: win `round(regular games × share)` (share by stars 1-6: .42 .50 .58 .66 .75 .83), beat the first protected rival, and by stars: 5-6 the conference title (title game winner, else the standings leader: `conferenceChampion`), 3-4 a top-N signing class (N 55 / 35 / 20 / 10 for 3-6 stars; the class signed before the season), 1-2 a bowl game (1994) or 6 wins (2026). Read from the dynasty, not stored.
- **Hot seat** (`hotSeat`): per finished season at his school, 38 − 8 × (regular-season wins − the win goal), ± 6 per other goal met or missed, −6 / +8 for a signing class at or well below the school's target; carried as 0.4 × last + 0.6 × this season. A coach already there when the dynasty started starts from his last real season's record against the goal; a new coach from 30. `tenure` counts his real seasons at the school before the dynasty too.
- **Firing** (`firingChance`): seasons 1-2 never; season 3 90% after a disaster (wins at or below max(1, goal − 6) and a class worse than 1.5 × target + 10, or none signed in the dynasty); from season 4, 0 below 55, then 0.9 × (heat − 55) / 35 (at most 90%), × (1 − 0.12 × (his stars − 1)). Your teams' coaches only if `Dynasty.yourCoachFired` isn't false (Offseason rules page). Tuned with `cli/carouselstudy.ts` (a 1994 dynasty with a full season: about 2.4 firings and 2.2 retirements of head coaches per offseason, 7 hires; after more seasons the heat builds up).
- **Retirement:** from 60, a chance of 4% + 4% a year past 60 (at most 90%), 1.5 × for a coach without a head coaching job.
- **XP** (`seasonXp`): 40 a regular-season win, 150 a postseason win, 400 the national title, 200 a conference title, 100 a goal met, 200 / 100 for a top-10 / top-25 class. Kept in `DCoach.xp`; for a coach without Manage XP, `autoSpend` spends it at once (each point on a rating picked with weight rating − 50 + 6, at least 7). Costs (`xpCost`): 40 below 70, 80 to 79, 150 to 89, 300 from 90. `spendXp` is the user's purchase (needs Manage XP). Aging: from 65, each rating −1 with 40% chance a year. Prestige (`nextCoachPrestige`): one star a season toward his OVR's level (86 / 78 / 70 / 62), a national title +1, fired −1.
- **The carousel** (offseason step 1, `OffseasonStep` 'carousel'; `startOffseason` runs `startCarousel` when the dynasty has coaches): XP, aging, prestige, retirements, firings (OffseasonEvents `coachRetired`, `coachFired`), offers to the coaches with Manage XP (`jobOffers`: open schools at his school's stars up to 2 more, where his OVR + 3 × stars reaches the school's bar 58 + 5 × stars − 3; three at most). The user's changes: `placeCoach` (a pool coach to a team; that team's coach to the pool), `fireCoach`, `createCoach` (any time, into the pool). `finishCarousel(d, picks, teams)`: the managed coaches' picks (and, for a coach of a user team, whether to add the new school and keep the old one), then the computer fills openings from the most prestigious school down: pool coaches, or head coaches of less prestigious schools whose OVR + 3 × stars reaches the bar (their job opens), scored by that plus a little randomness and an alma mater bonus; the user's teams' coaches and the managed coaches aren't hired away (`coachHired` events). Step then 'depth'. `cancelOffseason` restores the coaches from `carousel.before`. `simOffseason` runs `finishCarousel` with no picks.
- **Pages:** the carousel step (retirements, firings, offers with the school sheet, your changes, openings, then "Hire for every opening"), a summary on the later steps, goals and hot seat on team and coach pages, a Hot seat tab and Create a coach on the Coaches page, Manage XP on coach pages, the firing switch on the Offseason rules page, and the masthead's teams menu (`firstTeam`: pages that pick one of your teams start on it).
- **Tests:** `cli/coachchecks.ts`: goals and hot seat for every team, XP spending, the carousel (a fire and a placement by the user, every opening filled, one coach per team, the user's pick kept), and starting the offseason over restores the coaches.

### Part 3: what coaches do, former players, headshots, stories (`coachEffects.ts`, `coachLook.ts`)

- **Development** (`developmentBoost`, in `trainPlayers`): a growing player's gain × (1 + boost); boost = 0.1 × ((group rating − 70) + 0.5 × (Development − 70)) / 43.5 × prestige factor (0.85 + 0.075 × (stars − 1)), at most ±12%. Group: Passing for QB, WR, TE; Running for HB, FB and the line; Development for K and P; Defense for the rest.
- **Recruiting:** full recruiting: every point costs × (1 − `recruitingDiscount`) (0.1 × (Recruiting − 70) / 29 × prestige factor, −10% … +12%); random signing day: the school's prestige share + `recruitingLift` (±0.05).
- **Transfers (2026 only)** (`transferFactor`, in `transferChance` and `runPortal`): chance × (1 − 0.15 × (coach OVR − 70) / 29, 0.85-1.1); a team whose head coach changed since last season: +0.10 for every player, and a player whose old coach is another school's head coach goes there first if it would start him.
- **Former players** (in `startCarousel`): each player who left this offseason (graduated or pro) with chance 0.25% × position (QB 2, LBs 1.5) × (Awareness / 80)^6, at most 5%; graduates join the pool now, players who went pro wait in `Dynasty.coachQueue` 2-10 seasons. `coachFromPlayer`: ratings about 44 + 0.35 × (Awareness − 50) (+4 drafted), +8 in his side's rating, +4 Recruiting when drafted; prestige 1 (2 drafted); born about 22 years before he left. - **The sim effect** (`simNudges`, in `seasonRom`; `Dynasty.coachSim`, Offseason rules → Coaches, off by default; spec: "subtle and carefully implemented and tested"): each side ±1 OVR at most: offense by the mean of Passing and Running, defense by Defense, round((rating − the season's median head coach) ÷ 12) clamped to ±1, so the league doesn't move as a whole (about as many teams up as down). Written into the season ROM's OVR table only. `cli/coachsimstudy.ts` (a copy of the sim's touchdown rule with the shipped settings, field goals left out): +1 on both sides wins 56.3% against an equal team (+1 OFF alone 53.2%); a first try at ±2 per side from a fixed center of 72 moved teams up to 63% and pushed most coaches down (the data's median coach is below 72), so it was cut to ±1 around the median. With the real coaches: 1994 26 teams +1/+1 and 16 −1/−1, 2026 17 and 22; the best coach is worth about 0.8 wins in 12 games against equal teams.

## Upgrading to a newer Elevated version

User, 2026-11-20: a dynasty started on v2.1.0 should move to v2.1.1 ("I'd like to be able to upgrade midseason, if possible"); keep the original game in the browser ("yes, I think keeping the rom is probably a good idea"); mockups approved (offer after an import, the upgrade window with and without the original, a version that can't continue the season, the offseason's version choice, Manage's rows); "proceed" with: the upgrade counts as done only once a save from the new ROM is imported.

- **Why it works:** every season ROM is the dynasty's kept base ROM (IndexedDB, `saveRom` / `loadRom`) plus the dynasty. An upgrade replaces the kept ROM and builds the season's ROM again; the dynasty, the save and the players don't change.
- **Finding a newer version** (`core/dynasty/upgrade.ts` `findUpgrade`): the Elevated release list (`patches.json`, as Get started). The kept ROM's SHA-1 must be an older listed release of the edition (`PATCH_CONTENT`); the offer is the current release's patch with the same fatigue choice, and its build (serial, mid-season-from) from `KNOWN_BUILDS` by the release's version name. Test builds and the current release get no offer; "Upgrade from a file" (Manage) still works with any newer build.
- **When** (`upgradeTiming`): `free` between seasons (the offseason, or a new season not downloaded or played yet); `safe` when the running season's start build (last import's ELVB start serial, else the season's `romBuild`) is at least the new build's mid-season-from; `later` when not (the card says it waits for the offseason); `unknown` when the build isn't known before patching (checked once the ROM is in hand). The first season counts as running (it's played on the user's ROM).
- **The ROM** (`upgradeRomProblem`): the same edition, a manifest, nothing the app doesn't know (`newerRomMessage`), a free dynasty area, newer than the kept ROM, and not `later`. From the original game (SHA-1 = Get started's `ORIGINAL_SHA1`) the app patches the current release. The original game is kept once for all dynasties (`saveOriginal`, key `#original` in the ROM store): when Get started patches it, and when it's loaded for an upgrade; Manage can remove it.
- **Mid-season** (`applyUpgrade`): the season's `romBuild` becomes the new build and `DSeason.upgrade = { from, to, downloaded }` waits. The download keeps the season's file name (first season: the name of the ROM played on). The "download again" window (`romStale`) also asks while the new ROM isn't downloaded. At import: a save whose ELVB last build is `to` ends it (`ImportResult.upgraded`, the note says so); one still from `from` gets a warning and keeps it waiting. The mid-season check (`midSeasonProblem`) uses the new `romBuild`, so a save started on the old build and saved on the new one passes when the new build allows it. Not a roster version bump (the rosters are the same).
- **Between seasons:** the newest season's `romBuild` changes and its ROM must be downloaded (again); nothing waits.
- **UI** (`tools/dynasty/Upgrade.tsx`): `useUpgrade` (reads the kept ROM, the original and the list once), `UpgradeCard` at the top of Manage (hidden after "Not now" for that version: `Dynasty.upgradeLater`), the window (patch from the kept original, or a file), `VersionRows` in Manage → This season (version, upgrade buttons, the original game with Remove), a line on the What's next card (`DynCtx.upgradeLine`), `VersionPick` on the offseason's Download the ROM step (newest picked; Download opens the window).
- **Tests:** `npm run test:upgrade -- OLD_ROM NEW_ROM SAVE [ROM_1994]` (4 checks on v2.1.0 → v2.1.1-test3 with a v2.1.0 save; the new-ROM save is the same save with ELVB's last build changed). Browser at 1280 / 390 with a faked release list: a 2026 first-season upgrade from a file (the download's ROM = test3 byte for byte), and a 1994 dynasty simmed to its 1995 offseason (version choice, upgrade, season ROM built on test3). Not run in the browser: patching from the kept original (the same `applyListed` path as Get started).
- **Check for a new version** (user, 2026-11-27): the lists are read once a page load (`listOnce`); the button in Manage → This season clears them and reads again, then says what it found or that the list can't be read (`UpgradeInfo.unreachable`), with the time.
- **Experimental builds** (user, 2026-11-27: prompts for release candidates and test builds, "bury this at the bottom", "only show up mid season if a mid season upgrade is safe. And it should say that in the prompt"): `Dynasty.experimentalBuilds`: a checkbox last in Manage → This season → Game version (`VersionRows`), beside the check it changes (moved from the bottom of Rules, user 2026-11-27); turning it on runs the check. The app then also reads `experimental.json` from the Elevated repo (`EXPERIMENTAL_LIST`, same format as `patches.json`, newest first; each patch with `serial` and `midSeasonFrom` from its ROM's manifest, so the mid-season check works before patching; a release may have `notes`, a URL). `findUpgradeAny` picks the newest by build serial: the experimental list's newest build of the edition, the current release (also when the kept ROM is a test build, by its KNOWN_BUILDS serial; a release whose serial the app doesn't know yet wins), or nothing. `offerShown`: a test build shows only when the timing is `free` or `safe`; the card says "Safe to switch mid-season", What's next has the line; when it can't continue the season, Manage → This season says so and the offseason's last step offers it (tag "Test build"). The upgrade itself is the usual one (patch from the kept original game, or a file; `upgradeRomProblem` checks the new ROM's own manifest). The ROM lane publishes the list (H-064). Tests: `test:upgrade` 6 (experimental checks); browser with a faked list (rc.3 dynasty, rc.5 serial 127, mid 72 → card, line, button; mid 125 → no card or line, Manage says it waits).
