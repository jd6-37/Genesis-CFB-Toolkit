Bill Walsh College Football '95 Elevated is a free, fan-made mod of EA's 1994 Sega Genesis game. It comes as a patch: you apply it to your own copy of the game, and you get a new ROM to play. The game itself is not included, and this site can't send it to you.

## What you need

- **Your own copy of the game:** the USA release of Bill Walsh College Football '95, as a ROM file (2 MB, `.md`, `.bin` or `.gen`). The patcher checks that it's the right one.
- **Something to play it on:** RetroArch with the Genesis Plus GX core, another Genesis emulator that handles 4 MB games with save RAM, or real hardware with a flash cart such as the Mega EverDrive Pro.
- **A controller:** any Genesis pad plays the game. A **6-button pad** unlocks the mod's new controls (marked <Six /> in this manual).

## Pick an edition

Both editions have every gameplay change in this manual, alternate uniforms and the Dynasty app. They differ in teams, rosters and the postseason.

| | 1994 Season Edition | 2026 Season Edition |
|---|---|---|
| Teams | 107: every Division I-A team of 1994 | 138: every FBS team of 2026 |
| Conferences | The 11 conferences of 1994 | The 2026 conferences |
| Postseason | The SEC Championship Game, then the 19 bowl games of 1994 | Conference title games, then a playoff (12, 24 or 4 teams), a BCS title game, or none |
| Rosters | 1994 rosters, with real names where they are known | 2026 rosters with names, rated from College Football 27 |
| Rules | The original game's clock; a regular-season or exhibition game can end in a tie | Today's college clock, the two-minute timeout and overtime rules |
| Historic teams | 32, for Exhibition games | 32, for Exhibition games |
| Dynasty app | Yes, without the transfer portal | Yes, with the transfer portal |

## Patch the game

The current release is **v2.1.0**. It has five patches: the two editions, and three that change only a little of the original game (the fatigue fix and bug fixes, the fatigue fix alone, or no fatigue at all). This manual covers the two editions.

1. Open the [Mod Toolkit](/toolkit#patch) and its **Patch** tab.
2. Drop your ROM file on the page (a zip file works too). The Toolkit checks that it is the right ROM.
3. Under **What to add**, pick the edition. Press **Apply patch**, then **Download patched ROM**.

The Toolkit runs in your browser, so your ROM is not uploaded anywhere. You can also use your own BPS patcher: the `.bps` files are on the [releases page](https://github.com/jd6-37/Bill-Walsh-CFB-95-Elevated/releases). Apply a patch to the original ROM, not to a ROM that is already patched.

Known limitations: each team added to the original 36 plays one of the original game's fight songs, so most don't play their own.

## Starting the game

Load the patched ROM. After the EA SPORTS and licensing screens, the title screen shows ELEVATED in red under '95, and the edition under it. Press <Btn k="START" /> to go to Game Setup.

<Tip title="6-button pad in RetroArch">If <Btn k="X" />, <Btn k="Y" /> and <Btn k="Z" /> do nothing, open the Quick Menu, then Controls, Port 1 Controls, and set Device Type to MD Joypad 6 Button. The patched ROMs tell the emulator they support a 6-button pad, but some setups need this setting once.</Tip>

<Tip title="Menu music">Press <Btn k="X" /> on the title screen, Game Setup or the season screens to turn the menu music off or on <New /> <Six />. The game remembers the setting. Fight songs and sound effects still play.</Tip>
