Bill Walsh College Football '95 Elevated is a free, fan-made mod of EA's 1994 Sega Genesis game. It comes as a patch: you apply it to your own copy of the game, and you get a new ROM to play. The game itself is not included, and this site can't send it to you.

## What you need

- **Your own copy of the game:** the USA release of Bill Walsh College Football '95, as a ROM file (2 MB, `.md`, `.bin` or `.gen`). The patcher checks that it's the right one.
- **Something to play it on:** RetroArch with the Genesis Plus GX core, another Genesis emulator that handles 4 MB games with save RAM, or real hardware with a flash cart such as the Mega EverDrive Pro.
- **A controller:** any Genesis pad plays the game. A **6-button pad** unlocks the mod's new controls (marked <Six /> in this manual).

## Pick an edition

Both editions have every gameplay change in this manual. They differ in teams, rosters and the postseason.

| | 1994 Season Edition | 2026 Season Edition |
|---|---|---|
| Teams | 107: every Division I-A team of 1994 | 138: every FBS team of 2026 |
| Conferences | The 11 conferences of 1994 | The 2026 conferences |
| Postseason | The 19 bowl games of 1994 | Title games and the College Football Playoff (12, 24 or 4 teams), BCS 2, or none |
| Rosters | 1994, with real names where they are known | 2026, from College Football 27 ratings, with names |
| Dynasty app | No | Yes |

## Patch the game

1. Open the [Mod Toolkit](/toolkit#patch) and its **Patch** tab.
2. Drop in your ROM (a zip works too). The Toolkit checks it and shows what it can build.
3. Pick the edition and press the button to build it. Save the new ROM.

Your file never leaves your computer: the Toolkit runs in your browser. If you'd rather use a patcher you already have, the `.bps` files are on the [releases page](https://github.com/jd6-37/Bill-Walsh-CFB-95-Elevated/releases); apply one to the original ROM, never to one that's already patched.

## Starting the game

Load the patched ROM. After the EA SPORTS and licensing screens, the title screen shows ELEVATED in red under '95, and the edition under it. Press <Btn k="START" /> to go to Game Setup.

<Tip title="6-button pad in RetroArch">If <Btn k="X" />, <Btn k="Y" /> and <Btn k="Z" /> do nothing, open the Quick Menu, then Controls, Port 1 Controls, and set Device Type to MD Joypad 6 Button. The patched ROMs tell the emulator they support it, but some setups need this once.</Tip>

<Tip title="Menu music">Press <Btn k="X" /> on the title screen, Game Setup or the season screens to turn the menu music off or on <New /> <Six />. The game remembers your choice; fight songs and sound effects still play.</Tip>
