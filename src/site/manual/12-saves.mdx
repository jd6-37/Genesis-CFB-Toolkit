## Saves

The game saves your season, your audibles and the menu music setting in the cartridge's battery-backed save memory, or in your emulator's save file (`.srm` in RetroArch). Keep that file next to the ROM.

Both editions are 4 MB ROMs with 32 KB of save memory (the original game has 8 KB).

**Updating from v2.0.0:** v2.1.0 uses a new save layout, so your save resets once. Custom audibles go back to the defaults, and a season in progress is lost. Finish a season before you update.

## Emulators and flash carts

- **RetroArch with Genesis Plus GX:** the mod is tested on this setup.
- **Mega EverDrive Pro:** tested on real hardware, including a full 2026 season saved and reloaded.
- Other emulators should work if they handle 4 MB games with save memory.

## 6-button pads

The patched ROMs tell the emulator or console that they support 6-button pads. With a 3-button pad the game plays as before, without the controls marked <Six /> in this manual. In RetroArch, if <Btn k="X" />, <Btn k="Y" /> and <Btn k="Z" /> do nothing, set the port's Device Type to MD Joypad 6 Button (chapter 1).

## Checksums and Game Genie

Every patched ROM has a corrected checksum, so it doesn't need a Game Genie master code. The patcher checks your original ROM before it builds anything. To update to a new version, patch the original ROM again. Don't patch a ROM that is already patched.

## The apps

- The **[Mod Toolkit](/toolkit)** patches the game in your browser and edits a ROM: rosters (also from College Football 27 ratings), team swaps, offense and defense styles, Walsh Reports, alternate uniforms.
- **[Dynasty](/dynasty)** runs a dynasty over many seasons, for both editions: recruiting, player progression, head coaches and the coaching carousel, and schedules. Starting a dynasty, you set up its first season (coaches, rosters, conferences and schedule, or the defaults), and every season, the first one included, is played on a ROM the app writes.
