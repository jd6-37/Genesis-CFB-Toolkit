## Saves

The game saves your season, your audibles and the menu music setting in the cartridge's battery-backed save memory, or in your emulator's save file (`.srm` in RetroArch). Keep that file next to the ROM.

<Edition only="2026">

The 2026 Season Edition is a 4 MB ROM with 32 KB of save memory (the original game has 8 KB). A season saved with v1.1.0 doesn't carry over.

</Edition>

<Edition only="1994">

The 1994 Season Edition is a 4 MB ROM. Its season save is laid out for 107 teams, so seasons saved with earlier versions start over.

</Edition>

## Emulators and flash carts

- **RetroArch with Genesis Plus GX:** the reference setup the mod is tested on.
- **Mega EverDrive Pro:** tested on real hardware, including a full 2026 season saved and reloaded.
- Other emulators should work if they handle 4 MB games with save memory.

## 6-button pads

The patched ROMs tell the emulator or console that they support 6-button pads. With a 3-button pad the game plays as before; you just don't get the controls marked <Six /> in this manual. In RetroArch, if <Btn k="X" />, <Btn k="Y" /> and <Btn k="Z" /> do nothing, set the port's Device Type to MD Joypad 6 Button (chapter 1).

## Checksums and Game Genie

Every patched ROM has the game's built-in checksum corrected, so none needs a Game Genie master code. The patcher checks your original ROM before it builds anything. To update to a new version, patch the original ROM again; don't patch an already patched one.

## The apps

- The **[Mod Toolkit](/toolkit)** patches the game in your browser and edits a ROM: rosters (also from College Football 27 ratings), team swaps, offense and defense styles, Walsh Reports, alternate uniforms.
- **[Dynasty](/dynasty)** runs a multi-season dynasty for the 2026 Season Edition: recruiting, progression and schedules, written into a new ROM for each season.
