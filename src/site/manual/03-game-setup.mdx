## The rows

- **Play Mode:** *Exhibition* (one game), *New Season* (start a season, chapter 10) or *Continue Season* (go back to the saved one).
- **Home Team** and **Visitor Team:** the teams are in alphabetical order. Hold left or right to scroll fast through the list <New />.
- **Game Length:** 20, 40 or 60 minutes (5, 10 or 15 minutes a quarter). Longer games also mean more scoring chances when the season sim plays a game for you.
- **Weather:** Fair, Windy, Rain or Snow. Wind carries kicks, so aim for it. In a season, the home team's climate picks the weather.
- **Difficulty** <New />:

| Level | What the CPU does |
|---|---|
| Normal | Plays as in the original game |
| Smart | Audibles against your formation and leans toward stopping what's working for you |
| Elite | Smart, and +1 to the CPU team's ratings |
| Champion | Sharper adjustments, more audibles, +2 to the CPU team's ratings (+1 speed), faster-reacting defenders, and a CPU team that tires at half the rate |

In a New Season two more rows show up: **Schedule** and **Postseason** (chapter 10).

## OFF and DEF ratings

Under each logo, OFF and DEF are the team's offense and defense as modern-style overall ratings <New />. In the 2026 Season Edition they come from the 2026 rosters; in the 1994 edition, from EA's team ratings and the 1994 research. The same numbers show on Team Schedules, and the season sim plays games with them.

## Team Rosters from Game Setup

Press <Btn k="Z" /> <Six /> to look at a team's roster without starting a game: the highlighted team's, or the home team's. Left and right step through every team; <Btn k="C" /> switches between ratings and names; <Btn k="Z" /> or <Btn k="START" /> goes back. More in chapter 11.

## The controller screen

<Shot src="/media/manual/controllers.png" alt="The controller screen: a controller under each team, or in the middle for the CPU" />

Move your controller under the team you want with the D-pad left and right. A team with no controller under it is the CPU's: leave every controller in the middle to watch CPU vs CPU. With two controllers, each player takes a team.

**Uniforms** <New /> <Six />: press <Btn k="Y" /> under a team to pick its helmet, jersey and pants. Up and down choose the piece, left and right the option, <Btn k="Y" /> is done, <Btn k="START" /> plays. The visiting team is switched to the opposite jersey (dark or white) from the home team's. A CPU team picks its own unless you set it.

Press <Btn k="START" /> to go to the pregame show.
