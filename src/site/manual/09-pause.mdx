Press <Btn k="START" /> during a game to open the pause menu. Pick an item with the D-pad and press <Btn k="C" /> or <Btn k="START" />.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Resume Game | Back to the game (or press START again) |
| Timeout | Call a timeout. Elevated shows how many you have left, "Timeout - 2 left", right below Resume Game <New /> |
| Team Rosters | Both rosters with energy; C switches to names <New />. Elevated moved it up to right after Timeout <New /> |
| Replay-Normal, Replay-Reverse | Instant replay of the last play, seen from behind the offense (Normal) or from the other end (Reverse) |
| Drive Summary | Every play of the current drive |
| Game Stats, Team Stats | The game's statistics |
| Scoreboard | The score and the game situation |
| Play Call | 3 Box or 1 Box (chapter 6) |
| Pass Catch | Auto or Manual (chapter 6) |
| Pass Window | Off (windowless passing) or On |

## Timeouts

Each team has three timeouts a half. A timeout stops the clock and puts every player back at full energy, as halftime does. In Elevated, you call timeouts from the pause menu <New />. The play-call screen's TIME OUT tile is now SWAP HBS on offense and SWAP MLBS on defense.

<Edition only="2026">
**The clock** <New />: until the last 2:00 of each half, the clock keeps running after a first down and after a runner goes out of bounds. The clock stops at 2:00 in the 2nd and 4th quarters. After a timeout, you can't call another one before the next snap (Timeout is hidden). The game clock also runs a little slower than in the original game.
</Edition>

## Instant replay

The replay keeps the whole play <New />: in the original game it showed only the last 11 seconds or so. On long plays the replay is a little choppier after that.

While the replay runs: hold <Btn k="B" /> for slow motion, or tap it to go one frame at a time. <Btn k="C" /> plays at normal speed, and <Btn k="A" /> rewinds. The D-pad moves the camera around the field.
