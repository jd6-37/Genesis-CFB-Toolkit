Press <Btn k="START" /> during a game. Move with the D-pad and press <Btn k="C" /> or <Btn k="START" /> on an item.

<Shot src="/media/manual/pause.png" alt="The pause menu: Resume Game, Replay-Normal, Replay-Reverse, Team Rosters" />

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Resume Game | Back to the game (or press START again) |
| Timeout | Call a timeout. Elevated shows how many you have left, "Timeout - 2 left", right below Resume Game <New /> |
| Replay-Normal, Replay-Reverse | Instant replay of the last play, from behind the offense or the other way |
| Team Rosters | Both rosters with energy; C switches to names <New /> |
| Drive Summary | Every play of the current drive |
| Game Stats, Team Stats | The game's statistics |
| Scoreboard | The score and the game situation |
| Play Call | 3 Box or 1 Box (chapter 6) |
| Pass Catch | Auto or Manual (chapter 6) |
| Pass Window | Off (windowless passing) or On |

## Timeouts

Each team has three timeouts a half. A timeout stops the clock and puts every player back at full energy, as halftime does. Timeouts moved to the pause menu in Elevated <New />; the play-call screen's tile is now SWAP HBS / SWAP MLBS.

## Instant replay

While the replay runs, hold <Btn k="B" /> for slow motion or tap it for one frame at a time, <Btn k="C" /> plays at normal speed, and <Btn k="A" /> rewinds. The D-pad moves the camera's target over the field.
