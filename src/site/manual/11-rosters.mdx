## Names and classes

The names view shows each player's class (FR, SO, JR, SR, or SR+ for a redshirted senior) and name <New />. The 2026 Season Edition has a name for every player; the 1994 edition has the real 1994 names where they are known. In a dynasty season, the names come from the Dynasty app. Play recaps also show the players' jersey numbers and names, for example "Pass to #1 Derrick Mayes for 75 yards".

## Positions

The roster lists, in order: QB; HB1, HB2 and FB; the receivers SE, FL, WR3, WR4 and two tight ends; the line LT, LG, C, RG, RT; kicker and punter; then the defense: LE, DT1, DT2, RE; the linebackers LOLB, MLB1, MLB2, ROLB; and the defensive backs CB1 to CB4 and the safeties. CB1 and CB2 are the starting cornerbacks <New />.

## What the ratings do

Every rating has 16 steps, shown as 0 to 100. The 2026 Season Edition also stores finer values between the steps <New />. ENERGY is how fresh the player is; WT. is his weight in pounds. The other ratings depend on the position:

| Rating | Who has it | What it does in the game |
|---|---|---|
| SPEED | Everyone | Top speed |
| QUICK | Backs, receivers, QB, linebackers, defensive backs | How fast he gets to top speed |
| PWR | Linemen | How hard he pushes in a block or a pass rush |
| INTEL | Everyone | How fast he reacts and reads the play; for a quarterback, the accuracy of long throws |
| WT. | Everyone | Mass in collisions: heavier players push lighter ones back and keep their momentum |
| HANDS | Most positions | Catching, interceptions, holding on to the ball when hit, loose balls, blocking kicks |
| RANGE | Quarterbacks | How far he can throw (about 40 to 56 yards) |
| POWER | Ball carriers | Breaking tackles, staying on his feet after a hit |
| TACKLE | Defense | Making the tackle when he gets to the ball carrier |
| BLOCK | Offensive line | Staying on his feet after a hit. Blocking itself uses PWR, weight and speed |
| QUICK, ACCURACY | Kickers, punters | Leg strength (distance) and how straight the kick goes |

The details and the numbers are in the Insights post [What each player rating does in the game](/insights/player-ratings).

## Energy

ENERGY goes down based on what a player does during each play. At every snap, he recovers a quarter of the energy he has lost (chapter 6). Below about 94% energy, all his ratings except weight are lower; at 75% energy he plays at about 82% of his ratings. Timeouts and halftime reset everyone to 100%.

## Impact players

<New /> Every team has three impact players: its best offensive skill player, its best defender, and the better of the next two. Each player is compared with his own position group (quarterbacks, backs, receivers, tight ends, linemen, linebackers, defensive backs) on the ratings that matter there, INTEL included. A fullback is picked only when no halfback is. CPU teams have impact players too. In a dynasty, you can pick your own teams' three in the Dynasty app's offseason (at most two on offense or on defense); the season ROM carries them.

On Team Rosters they have a yellow dot between number and position, and the position in yellow. On the field, a white X marks them all game.

**In the zone.** A hot streak puts an impact player in the zone: his X turns into a flame X, and his key ratings and awareness go up.

| Player | Into the zone | Knocked out |
|---|---|---|
| Quarterback | 4 completions in a row, or a touchdown pass | A sack or an interception |
| Back | 3 runs of 4 yards or more in a row, or a run of 15 yards or more | A tackle for loss |
| Receiver or tight end | 3 catches on one drive, or a catch of 20 yards or more | A drop, or no catch in the next 5 plays |
| Defender | A sack, a tackle for loss or an interception | A touchdown allowed, or 2 first downs by the offense without a tackle or sack by him |

The zone lasts 8 plays. From the 8th play on, a play of his own or a big play by his offense keeps him in it one play longer. It also ends at halftime and at the end of the game.
