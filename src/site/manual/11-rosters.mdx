## Names and classes

The names view shows each player's class (FR, SO, JR, SR, or SR+ for a redshirted senior) and name <New />. The 2026 Season Edition has a name for every player; the 1994 edition has the real 1994 names where they are known. In a dynasty season, the names come from the Dynasty app. Play recaps name the players too: "Pass to #14, for 47 yards, tackle by #7", with their names.

## Positions

The roster runs QB; HB1, HB2 and FB; the receivers SE, FL, WR3, WR4 and two tight ends; the line LT, LG, C, RG, RT; kicker and punter; then the defense: LE, DT1, DT2, RE; the linebackers LOLB, MLB1, MLB2, ROLB; and the defensive backs CB1 to CB4 and the safeties. CB1 and CB2 are the starting cornerbacks <New />.

## What the ratings do

Every rating has 16 steps, shown as 0 to 100 (the 2026 Season Edition works in finer steps in between <New />). ENERGY is how fresh the player is; WT. is his weight in pounds. The rest depend on the position:

| Rating | Who has it | What it does in the game |
|---|---|---|
| SPEED | Everyone | Top speed |
| QUICK | Backs, receivers, QB, linebackers, defensive backs | How fast he gets to top speed |
| PWR | Linemen | Drive: how hard he pushes in a block or a rush |
| INTEL | Everyone | How fast he reacts and reads the play; for a quarterback, the accuracy of long throws |
| WT. | Everyone | Mass in collisions: heavier players push lighter ones back and keep their momentum |
| HANDS | Most positions | Catching, interceptions, holding on to the ball when hit, loose balls, blocking kicks |
| RANGE | Quarterbacks | How far he can throw (about 40 to 56 yards) |
| POWER | Ball carriers | Breaking tackles, staying on his feet after a hit |
| TACKLE | Defense | Making the tackle when he gets to the ball carrier |
| BLOCK | Offensive line | Staying on his feet after a hit (blocking itself is drive, weight and speed) |
| QUICK, ACCURACY | Kickers, punters | Leg strength (distance) and how straight the kick goes |

The full story, with the numbers, is in [Insights](/insights/who-wins-at-the-line).

## Energy

ENERGY drops with what a player does on each play and comes back a quarter at a time at every snap (chapter 6). Below about 94%, every one of his ratings is a little lower; at 75% he plays at about four fifths of his ratings. Timeouts and halftime bring everyone back to 100%.

## Impact players

<New /> Every team has three impact players, picked from the roster by position group: a yellow dot and a yellow position mark them on Team Rosters, and a marker shows over them on the field. When one of them gets into the zone during a game, he plays above his ratings for a while.
