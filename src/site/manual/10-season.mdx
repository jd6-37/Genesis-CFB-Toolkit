Set Play Mode to **New Season** on Game Setup to start a season, or **Continue Season** to go back to the saved one. A new season replaces the saved one.

## Starting a season

<Edition only="2026">

<Shot src="/media/manual/season-setup.png" alt="Game Setup for a new season: the Schedule and Postseason rows" />

- **Schedule:** *Random* (12 weeks: each conference's games plus non-conference games, dealt again every season) or *Real 2026* (the real 2026 schedule from Week 0; games against FCS teams are byes). A season the Dynasty app wrote into the ROM shows *Dynasty* here and plays its own schedule.
- **Postseason:** *CFP 12* (default), *CFP 24*, *CFP 4*, *BCS 2* or *None*.

</Edition>

<Edition only="1994">

- An 11-week season with the 107 teams of 1994 in their 1994 conferences.
- **Postseason:** *Bowls* (the 19 bowl games of the 1994 season) or *None*.
- The season starts from the real 1994 preseason AP poll.

</Edition>

## The season screens

Press <Btn k="START" /> on the Week Schedule for the season menu.

<Shot src="/media/manual/week-schedule.png" alt="The Week Schedule" />

- **Week Schedule:** one week's games. **Play Week** simulates every CPU game of the week; games with HOLD next to them wait for you to play them.
- **Team Schedules:** any team's schedule, results, and OFF and DEF ratings. <Btn k="C" /> sets all of the team's games to HOLD (you play them) or back to CPU, and makes it one of your teams.
- **Rankings:** the Top 25 and every conference's standings.
- **Season Stats, Team Stats, Team Rosters:** over all the season's teams.

<Edition only="2026">

- **CFP Bracket:** during the Playoff.

</Edition>

Ranked teams carry their rank in games: "#5 Georgia" in the score box and the timeout box, and under the team on the controller screen <New />.

## The postseason

<Edition only="2026">

**Conference title games** (every Postseason setting except None): the top two in each conference by conference record (then overall record, then rank) meet at the first team's home. The Independents have none.

| Postseason | Who plays |
|---|---|
| CFP 12 | The 2026 rules: the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12 and SEC champions, the best-ranked champion of the other conferences, Notre Dame if it's in the top 12, and the highest-ranked other teams. Seeded by rank; seeds 1 to 4 get a bye; first-round games are at the higher seed. |
| CFP 24 | The four champions above, the best-ranked other champion, and 19 more teams by rank |
| CFP 4 | The top 4: semifinals 1 vs 4 and 2 vs 3, then the final |
| BCS 2 | The top 2 meet in the BCS National Championship |
| None | Regular season only |

<Shot src="/media/manual/cfp-bracket.png" alt="The CFP Bracket screen" />

</Edition>

<Edition only="1994">

After week 11 the 19 bowls of the 1994 season fill their slots the way they did then: the Rose Bowl takes the Big Ten and Pac-10 champions, the Bowl Coalition fills the Orange, Sugar, Cotton and Fiesta, and the other bowls take their conference slots. A team needs 6 wins to go to a bowl. All the bowls are played in one week; each bowl's name shows on its Week Schedule row. The national champion is the No. 1 team in the final poll.

</Edition>

Playoff, title and bowl games can't end in a tie: they go to sudden-death overtime. If one of your teams wins the national title, the end-of-season celebration plays.

## The sim

When you don't play a game, the season sim plays it with each team's OFF and DEF ratings: a team's offense against the other team's defense, chance by chance, for four quarters. The original game read the two ratings backwards; Elevated fixed it <New />. How it works is in [Insights](/insights/season-sim).

## Saving

The season saves to the cartridge's save memory (or your emulator's save file) as you go. The 2026 Season Edition's 138-team season needs 32 KB of save memory; RetroArch's Genesis Plus GX and the Mega EverDrive Pro handle it. More in chapter 12.
