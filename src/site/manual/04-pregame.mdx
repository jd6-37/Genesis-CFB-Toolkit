Before every game, Bill Walsh welcomes you to the home team's stadium. In a season game, he says the rank of any ranked team: "Today it's UL Monroe against #5 Georgia" <New />.

<Shot src="/media/manual/pregame-menu.png" alt="The pregame menu: Start Game, The Walsh Report, Team Matchups, Team Rosters" />

Pick an item with up and down and press <Btn k="C" />. The list scrolls: the arrow at the bottom means there is more. <Btn k="START" /> brings you back to the menu from any item.

- **Start Game:** go to the coin toss.
- **The Walsh Report:** Walsh's scouting report on each team's offense and defense. In the 2026 Season Edition every team has its own report, written for 2026, naming the key players by jersey number <New />.
- **Team Matchups:** the two teams rated side by side, one position group at a time. The higher number is the stronger group. You can only see this screen here, before the game.
- **Team Rosters:** both rosters, with ratings and energy. Left and right switch teams, up and down scroll the positions. <Btn k="C" /> switches to names <New /> (chapter 11).
- **Scoreboard:** the scores of the day's other games; up and down scroll the list. The pause menu has it too.
- **Set Audibles:** choose the three plays your A, B and C audibles call, on offense and on defense (below).
- **Play Call, Pass Catch, Pass Window:** the same settings as on the pause menu (chapters 6 and 9). <Btn k="C" /> switches between the two choices.

A ticker below the stadium shows the scores of other games. In a season it also shows the teams' ranks.

## Setting your audibles

Each team has three audibles on offense and three on defense, one for each of <Btn k="A" />, <Btn k="B" /> and <Btn k="C" />. Your choices stay set until you change them, from game to game. A new save starts with these <New />:

| Button | Offense | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| <Btn k="A" /> | I-Form, Trp. Opt. R. | 4-4, Jet Blitz |
| <Btn k="B" /> | I-Form, WR Outs | 4-3, Medium Zone |
| <Btn k="C" /> | Shotgun, Deep Outs | Nickel, Full Zone |

A save made before this change keeps the audibles it has.

To change them:

1. Pick **Set Audibles**. Up and down pick the audible to change, for example offense B. Press <Btn k="C" />.
2. Pick a formation and then a play, the same way as on the play-call screen (chapter 6). The audible list comes back.
3. Pick **Exit**, or press <Btn k="START" />, to go back to the pregame menu.

In 1 Box mode you can make fake picks here too, so the player next to you can't tell which plays you set. During a game, Set Audibles is on the pause menu when you pause from the play-call screen.
