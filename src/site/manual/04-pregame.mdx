Before every game Bill Walsh welcomes you to the home team's stadium. When a ranked team plays in a season game, he mentions it: "Today it's UL Monroe against #5 Georgia" <New />.

<Shot src="/media/manual/pregame-menu.png" alt="The pregame menu: Start Game, The Walsh Report, Team Matchups, Team Rosters" />

Move with the D-pad and press <Btn k="C" /> or <Btn k="START" />:

- **Start Game:** go to the coin toss.
- **The Walsh Report:** Walsh's scouting report on each team's offense and defense. In the 2026 Season Edition every team has its own report, written for 2026, naming the key players by jersey number <New />.
- **Team Matchups:** the two teams side by side, position group by position group.
- **Team Rosters:** both rosters, with ratings and energy. <Btn k="C" /> switches to names <New /> (chapter 11).

Below the stadium, a ticker runs the scores of the other games around the country, with their ranks in a season.
