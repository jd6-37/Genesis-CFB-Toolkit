## The coin toss

<Shot src="/media/manual/coin-toss.png" alt="The coin toss: Heads or Tails" />

The visiting team calls the toss while the coin is in the air: press up or down to pick Heads or Tails, then <Btn k="C" />. The winner chooses to receive, to kick, or which end to defend. The other team then makes the other choice. To pick an end, press left or right, then <Btn k="START" />.

<Shot src="/media/manual/choose-end.png" alt="Choosing which end to defend" />

## Kicking off

1. Press <Btn k="C" /> to start the kick meter.
2. While the meter moves, aim with left and right. If there is wind, aim to make up for it.
3. Press <Btn k="C" /> again to kick. The higher the meter, the longer the kick.

The kicker's leg strength sets how far a full kick goes. On Team Rosters, leg strength is his QUICK rating (chapter 11).

**Onside kicks:** before the kick, press <Btn k="A" /> for the kickoff audibles and pick the onside formation. In Elevated, the player who fields an onside kick fumbles half as often when he is hit <New />, so the kicking team gets the ball back less often.

After a score, the players get to their kickoff spots faster than in the original game: about 5 seconds to the kick instead of 13 <New />. If the defense recovers a fumble on a 2-point try, the scoring team now kicks off <New />. In the original game, the wrong team kicked off.

## Returning a kick

The CPU controls the returner until you press a direction on the D-pad. After that, you control him.
