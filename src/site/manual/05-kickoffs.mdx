## The coin toss

<Shot src="/media/manual/coin-toss.png" alt="The coin toss: Heads or Tails" />

The visiting team calls the toss while the coin is in the air: up and down pick Heads or Tails, <Btn k="C" /> calls it. The winner chooses to receive, to kick, or which end to defend; the other team then makes the remaining choice. To pick an end, press left or right, then <Btn k="START" />.

<Shot src="/media/manual/choose-end.png" alt="Choosing which end to defend" />

## Kicking off

1. Press <Btn k="C" /> to start the kick meter.
2. Aim with the D-pad left and right while the meter moves. Allow for the wind.
3. Press <Btn k="C" /> again to kick. The higher the meter, the longer the kick.

The kicker's leg strength sets how far a full kick goes (on Team Rosters it's his QUICK rating, chapter 11).

**Onside kicks:** before the kick, press <Btn k="A" /> for the kickoff audibles and pick the onside formation. In Elevated the player who fields an onside kick fumbles half as often when he's hit <New />, so the kicking team gets the ball back less often, closer to real football.

After a score, the players run to their kickoff spots much faster than in the original game: about 5 seconds to the kick instead of 13 <New />. And if the defense recovers a fumble on a 2-point try, the scoring team kicks off, as the rules say <New /> (the original game had the wrong team kick).

## Returning a kick

The returner fields the ball and runs on his own. Press any direction on the D-pad to take him over.
