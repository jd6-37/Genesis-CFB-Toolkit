Field goals, punts and extra points are called from the **Special Teams** formation on the play-call screen. Fakes are there too.

## Kicking

Field goals, extra points and punts use the kick meter, as kickoffs do (chapter 5):

1. Press <Btn k="C" /> to start the meter.
2. Aim with the D-pad left and right. Watch the wind.
3. Press <Btn k="C" /> to kick. The higher the meter, the longer the kick.

A kicker's distance comes from his leg strength, and his ACCURACY rating sets how far a kick can drift off line (chapter 11).

## Defending a kick

On defense, the Special Teams formation has calls to return or rush a punt and to block a field goal. On a punt return the deep man fields the ball; take him over with the D-pad. To block a kick, get a man to the ball and press <Btn k="C" /> to jump.

## Extra points and two-point tries

After a touchdown, kick the extra point or run a play from the 3-yard line for two points. If the defense recovers a fumble on the try, the team that scored kicks off <New />: the original game made the wrong team kick.

In the season, playoff and title games can't end in a tie: they go to sudden-death overtime. Regular-season and Exhibition games can end tied, as in the original game.
